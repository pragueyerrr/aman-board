# Aman's Board

A personal job tracker built for my friend Aman, a creative professional looking for marketing and creative roles in Dubai.

---

## What is this?

It's a job board that scrapes Dubai-based creative and marketing roles and presents them in one focused place. No algorithm deciding what to show, no promoted listings, no noise — just real jobs in Aman's field, refreshed regularly.

Aman can also add any job he finds elsewhere on the internet: paste a URL and the app tries to autofill the details, or fill it in manually. The idea is one place to track everything, whether it came from the board or from his own searching.

## What does it do?

- **Scrapes fresh jobs** from multiple sources — Adzuna, Indeed, JSearch, and direct employer sites — filtered for creative, marketing, and content roles in Dubai
- **AI match scoring** — upload your CV and the app scores how well each job fits you, with a breakdown of strengths and gaps
- **AI resume generation** — one click generates a tailored resume for a specific job, downloadable as PDF or Word (.doc)
- **AI cover letter** — writes a personalised cover letter for each role, streamed in real time, downloadable as PDF or Word (.doc)
- **Save jobs** — bookmark anything worth tracking so it shows up in your Applications tab
- **Add your own jobs** — paste a URL from anywhere and the app autofills what it can, or add details manually

## Who is it for?

Aman. But honestly any creative or marketing professional job hunting in Dubai will find it useful.
